Privacy Policy for Balloon Pop!

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Balloon Pop! (the “App”), developed by Deena Nath Gupta (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when you use the App.

In brief: Balloon Pop! is designed to work offline. Based on the current version's intended setup, the App does not collect or send personal information to our servers, does not display advertisements, and does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask players to create an account, provide a name or email address, or submit personal information through the App. The App is designed to operate without an internet connection and does not transmit gameplay information to a server.

If the App stores progress or preferences (such as stars, unlocked themes, or settings), this information is stored locally on the device and is not sent to us.

2. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The App is intended to work offline and does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs in the current version. We do not receive personal information from the App.

This statement applies only if the published build and all included libraries, plugins, and services behave as described. If these components change, we will update this Privacy Policy and our Google Play disclosures as needed.

3. Children's Privacy

Balloon Pop! is designed for young children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. Because the App is designed to operate offline without transmitting gameplay data, we do not receive information about a child's gameplay from the App.

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Any game progress or preferences saved by the App remain on the user's device. You can generally remove this local data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. The exact effect depends on the device and its backup settings.

We do not maintain an App user database or server-side gameplay records for the current version.

5. Security

Because the App is designed not to transmit gameplay or personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side collection of such App data. Please keep your device and operating system updated and use the device's normal security features.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. We will publish the revised policy at this webpage and update the effective date. Please review this page periodically.
